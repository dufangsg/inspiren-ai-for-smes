How it works

Start with one process. Keep it only if it works.

We don't ask you to change your systems or sign a long contract. We take one piece of work, prove the result in a short pilot, then run it for you every month.

1

Free 30-minute process review

We ask how the process runs today, who touches it, how long it takes and what goes wrong. You get a short written summary with our estimate of the time it costs you.

2

Paid pilot, 4 to 8 weeks

We agree the scope, a fixed price and two or three success measures (for example, hours saved, turnaround time, error rate). We set up the workflow using your real documents, and your team reviews every output.

3

Monthly service

If the pilot meets its measures, we keep running the process for a monthly fee based on volume. You get a short monthly report. You can stop with one month's notice.

The daily work

Your team checks and approves. We do the drafting.

Documents and messages in
→
AI prepares a draft
→
Your team checks, corrects and approves
→

Corrections are fed back so drafts improve over time. The approval step is the first thing your eye lands on.

Our rules
  • A person on your side approves every output that leaves your business.
  • We only touch the data the process needs, and we tell you where it is stored.
  • Your data is used only for your service, never to train public AI models.
  • We handle personal data in line with Singapore's PDPA and work under an NDA.

Why us

Questions
Can you incorporate my company?

No. In Singapore, foreigners incorporate through an ACRA-registered filing agent. We prepare the documents and work alongside your filing agent, or introduce one.

Can you apply for Employment Passes?

No. Work pass applications are submitted by the employer or a licensed employment agency. We prepare the paperwork so it goes in complete.

Do you guarantee approval?

No. Every decision is made by the relevant agency or bank.

Do you work in Chinese?

Yes. Every translation is checked by a bilingual team member before it goes out.

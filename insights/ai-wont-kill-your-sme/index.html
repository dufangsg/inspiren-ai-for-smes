AI Won't Kill Your SME. A Smaller Competitor Using It Will!

Why Inspiren AI for SMEs exists, what we believe, and how we will earn our place in your business.

21 November 2026 · 5-minute read

The problem isn't the AI. It's the last mile.

Every SME owner in Singapore has now been told that AI will change their business. Most have tried it. Someone in the office uses a chatbot to tidy up an email. A manager watched a webinar. The company may even have paid for a few software licences.

And yet the progress claim is still assembled by hand at the end of the month. The doctor still finishes referral letters after the last patient. The admin team still re-types WhatsApp orders into a spreadsheet every morning.

The models are remarkable. What's missing is the last mile: the unglamorous work of taking one real process, with real documents, real exceptions and real deadlines, and making AI do it reliably, every week, inside a business that has no data team and no time to build one.

Large companies close that gap with in-house engineers, consultants and transformation budgets. SMEs are left with a login and a tutorial video.

We started Inspiren AI for SMEs to close that gap.

Our vision

Every SME in Singapore should run with the back office of a company ten times its size.

Not by hiring ten times the staff. Not by buying ten more systems. By handing the repetitive paperwork, chasing and checking to AI, run by a team that answers for the result, while the owner's own people keep the decisions.

A 30-person contractor should submit claims as cleanly as a listed builder. A two-doctor clinic should turn around reports as fast as a hospital. A trading company opening its first Singapore office should have its paperwork as organised as a multinational's. Size should stop being the reason a business is slow.

The top layer never changes hands. Everything below it is our job.

Five principles we run on

1. We sell finished work, not software.

An SME doesn't need another dashboard to log into. It needs the claim submitted, the letter signed, the order confirmed. So we don't sell licences and leave you to figure it out. We take one process and deliver its output, every month, and we're paid for that output. If the work isn't getting done, we haven't done our job, whatever the technology underneath.

2. We go to the work.

The best AI systems in the world weren't built in a lab. They were built by engineers who sat next to the people doing the job: on the site, at the front desk, in the filing room. We work the same way. Before we automate anything, we watch how the process actually runs: which documents arrive, in what state, from whom, and where it breaks. The exceptions are where the real work is, and you only find them by being there.

3. We map your business before we automate it.

AI is only as good as its understanding of your world. A progress claim is not just a PDF. It is a contract, a set of variations, delivery orders, a payment schedule and a main contractor who reads it a certain way. We build a working map of the things your business deals with (projects, patients, clients, orders, entities) and how they connect. That map is what lets AI do real work rather than produce plausible text. It also keeps getting more useful: every new process we take on builds on it.

4. Your people stay accountable.

AI drafts. People decide. A doctor signs every clinical letter. A Qualified Person submits every plan. A licensed agent files every incorporation. We design every workflow so a named person on your side approves what leaves the business, and so their corrections make the next draft better. In a regulated city, we think this is the only responsible way to use AI, and it is also how quality compounds.

5. We prove it in weeks, then earn it every month.

No long contract, no big-bang rollout. A free review to find the right process. A short, fixed-price pilot with two or three measures agreed up front: hours saved, turnaround time, error rate. If the numbers hold, it becomes a monthly service you can stop with a month's notice. We would rather earn your business every month than lock it in once.

What this looks like in practice

Every engagement follows the same path. It is deliberately small at the start, because trust in AI is earned one process at a time.

The first process is the hardest. The second is faster, because the map of your business already exists and your team already knows how to work with us. That is how a single pilot grows into a back office.

Where we start

We have chosen five areas where the paperwork is heavy, the rules are specific to Singapore, and the cost of getting it wrong is real:

  • Construction & Facilities: progress claims, safety documents, manpower data and submission packs.
  • GP Clinics and Hospitals: consult notes, referral letters, medical reports and patient follow-ups, signed by your doctors.
  • Professional Services: client onboarding files, recurring filings and first drafts from your own precedents.
  • Retail, F&B & Trading: orders, enquiries, invoice matching and price lists across WhatsApp and email.
  • Setting up in Singapore: document packs, registration dossiers, hiring paperwork and a bilingual back office for companies arriving from China and across Asia.

Different industries, same promise: one process, done properly, every month.

The work that matters stays human

We are not trying to replace the people who make your business work. We are trying to give them back the evenings they spend on paperwork, so they can spend them on customers, patients, projects and growth.

The companies that pull ahead in the next five years won't be the ones with the most AI tools. They will be the ones that quietly handed their routine work to AI and kept their best people on the work only people can do.

We would like to help you be one of them. Start with one process.

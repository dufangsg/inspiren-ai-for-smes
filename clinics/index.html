Safety and privacy
  • Your doctors stay in charge. AI drafts; doctors review, edit and sign every clinical document. The AI does not diagnose or advise patients.
  • Patient consent. Consultations are only processed with the patient's agreement, and the clinic can switch it off for any visit.
  • Built around Singapore's rules. We design each workflow with the Ministry of Health's guidance on AI in healthcare and the Personal Data Protection Act in mind.
  • Clear data handling. Before you start, we tell you in writing where data is stored, who can see it and how long it is kept.
